# Validation Metrics

What "validated" and "not validated" mean for this round of customer discovery. No interviews have been conducted yet as of this writing — this document defines the bar in advance so results can't be rationalized after the fact.

## Primary decision rule (from prior research, carried forward)

Run 8–10 discovery conversations (see [validation_plan.md](validation_plan.md)).

- **Go deeper on the initial segment (B2B/tech PR agencies)** if at least 3 of the interviewees independently describe late payment as a **recurring** and **costly** problem — not a one-off annoyance, and not something they've already fully solved.
- **Otherwise**, treat the initial segment as weak signal and test the media/performance-agency segment (fronted-cost hypothesis, H5) next.

## What counts as a "recurring and costly" signal

Both of these, from the interviewee's own words, not inferred:
- Recurring: happens more than occasionally (e.g., "most months," "a few clients every quarter"), not a single anecdote.
- Costly: has caused a concrete consequence — cash-flow strain, staff time, a client relationship cost, or a specific dollar/pound impact they can name.

A story that is vivid but one-off, or frustration without a concrete consequence, does not count toward the 3-of-10 threshold.

## What counts as disconfirming

- Interviewee says AR/collections is already handled well by an existing tool or person and describes no real friction.
- Interviewee cannot recall a specific instance of late payment causing a problem when asked directly.
- Interviewee explicitly says relationship risk from chasing is not a concern (undercuts H4).

## Secondary signals to log even if not decisive

- Which of H1–H5 came up unprompted vs. only after a direct question.
- Whether QuickBooks/Xero AI reminder features (see [competitive_landscape.md](competitive_landscape.md)) came up unprompted as "already handled."
- Any reaction to the words "triage," "context," or "promise-to-pay tracking" if you describe them (see [positioning_hypotheses.md](positioning_hypotheses.md)) — but only after the interviewee has described their own process first, to avoid leading them.

## Explicitly out of scope for this round

- Pricing validation — no pricing should be tested in these calls (see [pricing_research.md](pricing_research.md), [paid_pilot.md](paid_pilot.md)).
- Any claim of product-market fit. This round tests problem existence and severity only.
