# Customer Discovery Guide

Interview guide for the validation calls described in [validation_plan.md](validation_plan.md). Goal: find out whether late payment / overdue-invoice handling is a recurring, costly problem for the target segment — not to pitch CashPilot.

## Ground rules

- These are **research-only** conversations. Do not pitch, demo, or price anything unless the interviewee asks.
- Do not record or reuse company names/details outside the interview scorecard (see [interview_scorecard.csv](interview_scorecard.csv)) without the interviewee's knowledge.
- No real customer data of ours or theirs should be exchanged.

## What we're testing (see [validation_metrics.md](validation_metrics.md) for the pass/fail bar)

The five hypotheses from prior research:

1. Nobody clearly owns overdue invoices; finance lacks account context.
2. Retainers are billed in advance but paid late while service continues.
3. Promised-payment dates, PO issues, and disputes are hard to track.
4. Account leads avoid aggressive chasing because of relationship risk.
5. Fronted media/service costs create cash-flow pressure when invoices are late.

## Opening

"We're researching how agencies like yours handle overdue client invoices — not selling anything today. Mind if I ask a few questions about how that works here?"

## Core questions

**Ownership & process**
- Who notices when a client invoice is overdue, and who decides what happens next?
- Walk me through what happens between "invoice sent" and "invoice paid" today.
- Do you use anything (spreadsheet, accounting software reminders, a person) to track this?

**Pain & frequency**
- In the last quarter, roughly how many invoices went more than 30 days overdue?
- What's the most frustrating part of chasing a client for payment?
- Has a late payment ever caused a real cash-flow problem for the business?

**Relationship risk (H4)**
- Does anyone hesitate to chase a client because of the relationship? Tell me about a time that happened.

**Context tracking (H1, H3)**
- When a client says "we'll pay after the PO clears" or disputes part of an invoice, how is that tracked so nobody forgets or double-chases?

**Fronted costs (H5, if applicable — media/production agencies)**
- Do you ever pay costs upfront on a client's behalf (media spend, freelancers, production)? What happens to cash flow if that client pays late?

**Closing**
- If you could wave a wand and fix one thing about getting paid on time, what would it be?
- Is there anyone else here (finance, ops) worth talking to about this?

## After the call

Log the outcome in [interview_scorecard.csv](interview_scorecard.csv) immediately — segment, fit tier, whether late payment was described as recurring *and* costly (the specific bar in [validation_metrics.md](validation_metrics.md)), and a one-line quote if one was offered.
