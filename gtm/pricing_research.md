# Pricing Research

Research date: 2026-09-29. This document distinguishes **verified competitor prices** (official vendor sources) from **CashPilot pricing hypotheses** (not validated by any customer). See [competitive_landscape.md](competitive_landscape.md) for full sourcing detail and dates.

## Verified competitor pricing (as published by vendors, 2026-09-29)

| Vendor | Model | Published price |
|---|---|---|
| Chaser | Revenue-tiered flat monthly fee | £199–£899+/mo (~$259–$1,169 USD), by annual revenue band ([source](https://www.chaserhq.com/pricing)) |
| Kolleno | Per-user, turnover-tiered | £545–£1,245/user/month depending on tier and annual vs. monthly billing ([source](https://www.kolleno.com/pricing)) |
| Upflow | GIV-tiered, custom quote | No published dollar figures; tiers banded by annual Gross Invoice Value, unlimited seats ([source](https://upflow.io/pricing)) |
| Gaviti, Tesorio | Custom quote | No official pricing published |
| QuickBooks / Xero AI reminders | Bundled | No separate price; included in existing accounting-software subscription |

## What this tells us

- Standalone AR tools in this space price by company size (revenue or seats), not by invoice volume or a percentage of what's recovered.
- None of the surveyed standalone vendors publish pricing aimed at a 5–30 person agency specifically — Chaser's lowest tier (<£4M revenue) is the closest fit but still assumes a business larger than our smallest target.
- The embedded incumbents (QuickBooks, Xero) offer AI reminders at effectively no incremental cost, which sets a strong "why would I pay extra" bar for any paid product in this space — reinforcing why [positioning_hypotheses.md](positioning_hypotheses.md) can't rely on reminder-drafting alone.

## CashPilot pricing — hypotheses only, not validated

See [paid_pilot.md](paid_pilot.md) for structure options (A: one-off review, B: 30-day pilot — default, C: 6-week pilot). No dollar figures have been tested with any prospect. Do not treat anything in this section as a price to quote.

**Do not default to percentage-of-recovered-cash pricing.** It wasn't supported by prior research and is not adopted here without dedicated validation — it changes the sales conversation significantly (from "pay for a tool" to "pay us a cut of collections") and needs its own discovery, not an assumption carried over from competitor research.

## Open questions for future pricing research

1. Would this segment pay a flat monthly fee comparable to Chaser's lowest tier, given they're smaller than Chaser's minimum stated target?
2. Is per-seat pricing (Kolleno's model) workable for an 8–35 person agency where often only one person touches AR?
3. Does the "included free in QuickBooks/Xero" baseline mean CashPilot needs to be priced as an add-on to an existing workflow rather than a replacement tool?

None of these are answered by this research pass — they should be tested only after segment validation ([validation_metrics.md](validation_metrics.md)), not during initial discovery calls.
