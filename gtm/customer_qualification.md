# Customer Qualification

Working rubric for scoring prospects. Carried forward from the prior research session's fit-tier system (see [prospects.md](prospects.md)); not re-validated against real interviews yet.

## Ideal customer profile (hypothesis)

Owner-managed B2B agency, roughly 8–35 people, billing monthly retainers to mid-market/enterprise clients, where finance is handled by the owner/MD or a single finance person (not a finance team).

## Target segments

- Marketing agencies
- Creative agencies
- Software/dev shops
- Consulting firms
- Design studios
- PR agencies
- Production agencies

## Fit tiers

| Tier | Definition | Signal strength |
|---|---|---|
| A-fit | Matches the ICP closely: right size, retainer billing, owner/single-finance-person structure, visible signs of B2B service delivery | Strong |
| B-fit | Matches most ICP criteria but one dimension is uncertain (e.g., size unclear, billing model unconfirmed) | Moderate |
| C-fit | Loosely plausible but missing key ICP signals | Weak — deprioritize |

## Verification confidence

Separate from fit tier — how confident we are in the data used to assign the tier:

| Confidence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| High | Size, structure, and billing model confirmed from multiple public sources |
| Medium | Some signals confirmed, others inferred |
| Low | Mostly inferred from limited public information |

## Qualification questions (to confirm or revise fit during discovery)

1. Roughly how many people are in the business, and how many touch client billing or collections?
2. Are most clients billed on retainer, project, or a mix?
3. Who currently owns chasing an overdue invoice — is it one person, split across account leads, or nobody specific?
4. Roughly how many open invoices does the business carry at a given time?

## Known gap

None of this has been tested against real conversations yet. Treat tier assignments as a triage heuristic for outreach prioritization, not a validated segmentation. See [validation_plan.md](validation_plan.md) for how we intend to test it.
