# Outreach Hypotheses

Hypotheses about how to reach the target segment — untested. No outreach has been sent as part of this documentation session.

## Channel hypotheses

| Channel | Hypothesis | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Direct personalized email to generic business inbox | Will get a low but nonzero response rate for a research-only (non-salesy) ask | Untested |
| LinkedIn direct message to owner/MD | Higher response rate than generic email for owner-managed agencies, but harder to source at volume | Untested |
| Warm referral through existing network | Highest expected quality and response rate, lowest volume | Untested |
| Cold outreach citing a specific, verifiable pain point (e.g., "retainer billed in advance, paid late") | Risk: may come across as presumptive if wrong; per [prospects.md](prospects.md), company-specific pain evidence is thin, so this should stay generic to the segment, not claimed about the specific company | Untested |

## Message framing hypotheses (research-only ask)

- Framing as "we're researching how agencies like yours handle overdue invoices" (used in [customer_discovery_guide.md](customer_discovery_guide.md)) is hypothesized to get a better response than framing as "we built a tool, can we show you" — because it lowers the ask and doesn't require them to sit through a pitch.
- No AI/automation language in the initial outreach ask — introduce that only if the conversation gets there, to avoid pre-empting the discovery questions with a solution.

## What we are not doing

- No outreach at volume until the 8–10 conversation round (see [validation_plan.md](validation_plan.md)) is complete.
- No claims in outreach copy about traction, other customers, or results — there are none yet.
