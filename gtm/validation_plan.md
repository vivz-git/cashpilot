# Validation Plan

## Immediate next step

Hand-write personalized, research-only interview requests to:
- 7 UK B2B PR agencies (on retainer, per the ICP in [customer_qualification.md](customer_qualification.md))
- 3 US boutique agencies

Target: 8–10 completed conversations, using [customer_discovery_guide.md](customer_discovery_guide.md).

## Decision rule

See [validation_metrics.md](validation_metrics.md) for the exact bar. Summary: ≥3 of 10 must independently describe late payment as recurring *and* costly to justify going deeper on the PR-agency segment. Otherwise, pivot outreach to media/performance agencies (fronted-cost hypothesis, H5).

## Constraints on this round

- No outreach has been sent yet — this plan describes intended outreach, it does not confirm any has happened.
- No pricing or product pitch in these calls.
- No real customer data of ours is used or shared.
- Contacts must come from the (currently missing) prospect dataset once recovered, or freshly sourced — see [prospects.md](prospects.md).

## Sequencing after this round

1. If PR-agency segment validates → deepen discovery there, begin testing [positioning_hypotheses.md](positioning_hypotheses.md) language and, later, [paid_pilot.md](paid_pilot.md) structures.
2. If it doesn't → run the same guide against media/performance agencies.
3. Either way, log every conversation in [interview_scorecard.csv](interview_scorecard.csv) and dated entries in [research_log.md](research_log.md).

## Explicitly not part of this round

Paid pilots, pricing conversations, outreach at scale, or any claim of validated demand. This is a problem-existence check on a small sample.
