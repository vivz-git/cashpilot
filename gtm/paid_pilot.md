# Paid Pilot

Pricing/structure **hypotheses**, not validated market prices. Do not quote any of these figures to a prospect as settled pricing — see [customer_objections.md](customer_objections.md) and [validation_plan.md](validation_plan.md) for why pricing isn't discussed before segment validation.

## Candidate structures

| Structure | Description | Status |
|---|---|---|
| A. One-off overdue invoice review | A single, bounded engagement: review the current overdue-invoice book, identify triage/priority and context gaps, hand back recommendations. Lowest commitment, easiest first "yes." | Hypothesis |
| B. 30-day assisted pilot | **Default hypothesis.** Live use of CashPilot for 30 days with hands-on support, covering the natural flow of invoice → analysis → draft → approve & send → outcome tracking for one billing cycle. | Hypothesis (default) |
| C. 6-week hands-on pilot | Longer pilot for sensitive or high-value accounts where a single billing cycle isn't enough to observe promise-to-pay/dispute patterns, or where the prospect wants closer support before trusting AI-drafted comms. | Hypothesis |

## Why B is the default hypothesis

30 days roughly matches a typical monthly billing/reminder cadence (see [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md) D51: next-follow-up defaults to 7 days after a send), long enough to see at least one full follow-up cycle without asking for a long commitment upfront.

## Pricing model

**Do not default to percentage-of-recovered-cash pricing.** That model is not supported by any research to date — see [pricing_research.md](pricing_research.md). It's tempting because it's easy to justify ("we only get paid if you get paid"), but it hasn't been validated against what this segment actually wants (a flat/predictable tool cost vs. a cut of recovered revenue), and it would need its own discovery before adopting.

Until pricing research says otherwise, treat pilot pricing as **flat-fee, time-bounded**, matching structures A/B/C above.

## What triggers moving from discovery to a pilot conversation

Only after segment validation per [validation_metrics.md](validation_metrics.md) — pilots are not offered during the initial 8–10 discovery calls.
